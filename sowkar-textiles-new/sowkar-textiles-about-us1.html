WHO WE ARE

More Than a Uniform.
A Standard of
Professionalism.

SOWKAR TEXTILES & UNIFORMS is a Mangalore-based uniform manufacturing house serving healthcare, education, corporate, hospitality and industrial sectors across India.

We believe a uniform is not just fabric stitched together. It is the first impression of an institution, the daily comfort of a professional, and the silent signature of an organisation.

What sets us apart is not what we say — it is what we build. A 25,000+ sq.ft. production facility, 100+ advanced machines, and a team of 150+ skilled craftspeople working with one constant focus: fit, finish and fabric.
