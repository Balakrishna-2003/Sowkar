PRECISION WORKFLOW

INTELLIGENT MANUFACTURING HUBS

From raw bolt roll intake to final sealed delivery, every garment travels through specialized engineering hubs designed for flawless dimensional conformity.

01 / STAGE

Computerized CAD & Pattern Layout

Digital pattern engineering ensures zero-variance size consistency across thousands of pieces. Computerized fabric nesting optimizes yardage with minimal scrap generation while maintaining uniform grain alignment.

  • Automated grading across complete size charts
  • Laser-guided contour precision cutting
  • Verified fabric shrinkage compensation
02 / STAGE

Modular Stitching & Embroidery Hub

Purpose-built modular assembly lines handle distinct product sectors—including healthcare scrubs, industrial boiler suits, and formal corporate blazers—powered by high-tension multi-needle stitching heads.

  • Heavy-duty stress-point bar-tacking
  • Computerized multi-head corporate embroidery
  • Double-stitched reinforced structural seams
03 / STAGE

Steam Finishing & Dispatch Terminal

Finished garments undergo multi-point inspection before proceeding to industrial vacuum form press tables and sanitizing steam tunnels, emerging completely wrinkle-free and sealed for nationwide institutional distribution.

  • Vacuum form steam pressing & sanitization
  • Colorfastness & thread integrity clearance
  • Barcode-indexed institutional batch packing
