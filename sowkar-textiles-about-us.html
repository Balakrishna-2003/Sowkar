WHO WE ARE

CRAFTING IDENTITY. WEAVING EXCELLENCE.

Sowkar Textiles and Uniforms is a leading uniform manufacturer and dress designer in South India. Originally known as Sowkar Textiles and Tailors, the business was taken over by Prakash Bhandary Kuthethur and Joel Lavee D Souza, who renamed it and continue to drive its vision of quality, timely supply, and customer service. We source only the finest fabrics and materials to ensure durable, flawless garments, serving customers across South India and in export markets.

We also empower rural women from Madanthyar and Vamanjoor by providing stitching training and employment. Today, Sowkar is recognized as one of India's leading uniform designers and manufacturers, and the best uniform company in Mangalore. Through continuous innovation and upgradation, we deliver stylish uniforms for Hospitality, Authentic Workwears, and Medical Apparel—setting us apart from the rest.
